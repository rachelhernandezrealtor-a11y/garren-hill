# Rachel Studio Selective SEO Strategy

Date: 2026-05-03

Status: active platform module.

Purpose: make Rachel Studio property sites discoverable by the right people, credible to search engines and AI search, and still selective enough that the experience feels private, editorial, and rare.

## The Core Idea

The goal is not maximum traffic.

The goal is qualified discovery.

The right buyer, advisor, journalist, preservation person, Sotheby's contact, or referring agent should be able to find the work, trust the work, and feel the work was made for someone with discernment.

The public page creates desire and authority.

The private layer closes the room.

## The Law

Search strategy must serve the property truth.

Do not keyword-stuff.
Do not inflate.
Do not invent.
Do not make the page feel like a commodity listing.
Do not expose private or sensitive material for SEO.

Truth is the seduction.

## Visibility Model

### 1. Public Indexed Page

Purpose: attract and validate qualified searchers.

This page should be crawlable, indexable, fast, responsive, source-backed, and visually complete.

It includes:

- editorial property story
- key facts in visible text
- source-backed historical claims
- approved image/video assets
- structured data that matches visible content
- source links where editorially appropriate
- inquiry path

### 2. Public Authority Layer

Purpose: help Google, AI search, press, and human researchers understand why the property matters.

This can include restrained public pages or sections for:

- in print
- source notes
- preservation context
- architect/restoration context
- property video transcript
- press/backlink references

These should be elegant, not blog-spam.

### 3. Private Noindex Dossier

Purpose: protect selectivity and give serious people the deeper material.

This layer can include:

- floor plans
- disclosure documents
- deeper photo albums
- source binder
- detailed showing packet
- agent-to-agent materials
- alternate buyer-specific briefings

Rules:

- use `noindex` for private material
- do not rely on robots.txt alone for sensitive files
- do not let private PDFs leak metadata into search
- require Rachel approval before anything sensitive is public

## Buyer Search Psychology

Rachel Studio should map keywords to the way serious people actually arrive.

### Identity Searches

They already know the property or address.

Examples:

- Garran Hill
- 200 Hollycrest Drive
- Hollycrest Pinehurst
- Walter Hines Page house

Goal: own the exact identity query.

### Intent Searches

They want a property like this.

Examples:

- Pinehurst historic estate
- Pinehurst luxury estate
- historic estate in Pinehurst NC
- Neo-Georgian estate North Carolina
- estate home near Pinehurst Country Club

Goal: appear as the rare answer, not as another listing.

### Authority Searches

They are validating history, provenance, or cultural weight.

Examples:

- Walter Hines Page Pinehurst
- Walter Hines Page Garran Hill
- Ambassador to the Court of St James Pinehurst
- Doubleday founder Pinehurst home
- Pinehurst preservation estate

Goal: make the property legible to researchers and AI search.

### Visual Searches

They are searching by architecture, feeling, or detail.

Examples:

- Neo-Georgian brick estate
- Pinehurst historic home gate
- mahogany staircase historic home
- Delft tile fireplace
- restored 1916 estate
- Pinehurst estate grounds

Goal: make every approved image work as an indexable asset.

### Action Searches

They are ready to contact or show.

Examples:

- Rachel Hernandez Sotheby's Garran Hill
- Garran Hill private showing
- 200 Hollycrest Drive listing
- Sotheby's Pinehurst historic estate

Goal: make the path to Rachel unmistakable.

## AI Search / Answer Engine Strategy

Google says its AI search features use normal SEO fundamentals: crawlable pages, indexable text, useful content, page experience, high-quality images and videos, and structured data that matches visible content.

Rachel Studio should therefore optimize for AI search by making the page excellent for humans and explicit for machines.

Every property page should contain a visible, elegant factual spine:

- property name
- address or area
- price, if public
- property type
- architectural identity
- date built
- known historical association
- square footage / acreage / beds / baths, if public
- agent/brokerage
- source-backed citations

Do not hide important facts only in images, video, or visual effects.

## Structured Data Strategy

Use JSON-LD.

Markup should help machines understand what a careful human can already see.

Minimum graph for a public property site:

- `WebSite`
- `WebPage`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `SingleFamilyResidence`
- `Offer`
- `RealEstateAgent` or the most accurate local business/person representation available
- `ImageObject`
- `VideoObject` when video is public and important
- `Organization` or brokerage identity where appropriate

Rules:

- structured data must match visible page content
- do not mark up claims that are not on the page
- do not add fake ratings or reviews
- do not use Product markup unless the use is reviewed and appropriate
- validate before launch

## Image SEO Strategy

Every final image should have:

- approved Cloudinary public ID
- stable delivery URL
- descriptive alt text
- visible section assignment
- desktop crop
- mobile crop
- width and height
- responsive `srcset` or equivalent responsive delivery
- `f_auto,q_auto` or approved quality transformation
- image object metadata where useful

Alt text should describe the image honestly.

It should not be a keyword dumpster.

Example shape:

`Mahogany staircase inside Garran Hill, a 1916 Neo-Georgian estate in Pinehurst, North Carolina.`

## Video SEO Strategy

If video is public, it should have:

- crawlable landing page
- stable thumbnail
- visible title
- visible description
- `VideoObject` structured data
- duration, upload date, thumbnail URL where available
- transcript or scene summary where editorially appropriate
- consistent metadata across page, sitemap, and social tags

Remotion should eventually generate:

- property reel metadata
- social titles
- short descriptions
- video transcript blocks
- thumbnail options

## Backlink Strategy

Backlinks should be earned through authority, not spam.

Priority backlink lanes:

1. Sotheby's property ecosystem, where permitted.
2. MLS and syndication surfaces, where permitted.
3. PineStraw and local publication references.
4. Village Heritage / preservation references, where safe.
5. Matterport and visual tour references.
6. Rachel Hernandez professional profiles.
7. Social video posts that link back to the canonical property page.
8. Future editorial campaign pages that cite the property page as the source.

Every backlink target needs:

- source URL
- contact or platform
- desired link target
- anchor text
- claim supported
- risk warning
- status

No private names.
No unsafe claims.
No low-quality directory spam.

## The Velvet Rope

Selective does not mean invisible.

Selective means the public page is findable, but the deeper experience is controlled.

Public:

- story
- authority
- imagery
- provenance
- inquiry

Private:

- full dossier
- deeper docs
- sensitive files
- showing strategy
- buyer-specific briefing
- agent packet

This is how Rachel Studio can feel like a private room while still being legible to Google, AI search, press, and serious buyers.

## Measurement

Do not measure success by raw traffic alone.

Measure:

- qualified search impressions
- clicks for identity and authority queries
- image search impressions
- video search impressions
- time on page
- source link interaction
- inquiry quality
- referring agent interest
- backlinks earned
- Search Console coverage health
- Core Web Vitals and mobile usability

The goal is not "more people."

The goal is "the right person says yes, finally this."
