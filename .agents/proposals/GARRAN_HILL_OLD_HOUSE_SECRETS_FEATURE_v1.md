## 1. RECOMMENDED PLACEMENT ON HOMEPAGE

**Primary recommendation: Breath section after the Restoration Record**

The narrative logic is:
> Arrival → History → The House → Rooms → Restoration Record → **Old-House Secrets** → Grounds → Closing

The Restoration Record establishes that Garran Hill was methodically cared for. The Secrets section immediately after says: *and it still has things left to discover.* That is the payoff — restoration as context, mystery as reward.

**Secondary placement (if primary is too late in the scroll):**
Between the staircase section and the dining room. The staircase is where the secret coat closet lives — the reveal is in the architecture as you read about it.

**Do NOT place:**
- In the hero / above the fold (too clever too soon)
- As the final section (the closing owns that moment)
- Inside the gallery (gallery is visual browsing, not narrative)

**Format:** Compact breath section — short headline, 1 tight paragraph, optional 2–3 image grid or single cinematic image. Gold hairline above and below. Act-break label: "The Details."
