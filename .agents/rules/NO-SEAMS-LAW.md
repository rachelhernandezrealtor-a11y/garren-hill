# NO SEAMS BETWEEN SECTIONS — EVER (Rachel, 2026-06-29)

The page is ONE continuous editorial scroll. There are NO visible breaks
between sections — no divider lines, no gold rule under a chapter title, no
hard edges between blocks, no abrupt background changes that read as a seam.

- The `.chapter-mark .seam` gold hairline is KILLED (display:none). Do not
  reintroduce it anywhere.
- Sections share one continuous dark `--ink` ground and flow into each other.
  Where eras change (the gate seam), they DISSOLVE — they do not cut.
- "Looked at as a whole": the buyer experiences one unbroken descent, not a
  stack of separated cards. Every transition is a flow, never a seam.

This is law. It supersedes any older layout that used divider rules.
