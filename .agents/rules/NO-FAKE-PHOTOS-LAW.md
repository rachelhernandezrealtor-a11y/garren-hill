# NO FAKE PHOTOS LAW (Rachel, 2026-06-29)

We do NOT fake photos. Ever.

- NEVER AI-generate, composite, or synthesize a "matched frame" of the property to make a dissolve/time-lapse line up.
- NEVER replace a real on-site photograph with a generated one.
- Every photo on a Rachel Studio property site is a REAL photograph of the REAL place.
- AI tools (Kling, FLUX, etc.) are for MOTION/cinematic treatment of real footage and for non-photographic craft, NOT for inventing property imagery that pretends to be real.

If two real photos don't share a frame, the answer is a different EDITORIAL move (cut, separate beats, sequence them honestly) — NOT a fabricated photo to force a crossfade.

This is the same family as the artifact law: real-but-honest beats fake-but-pretty, always.
